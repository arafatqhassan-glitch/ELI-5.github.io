ELI 5 Client Advanced

A standalone, high-performance Android voxel client engineered for mobile gaming and Android TV.

📥 Download Latest APK

Key Highlights

📱 Independent Package Space

Runs under client.eli5.game, allowing you to install and play alongside standard builds without conflicting with existing game data.

⚡ Engine Optimization

Built on a tailored 5.17.0 engine release to maximize mobile frame rates, smooth out rendering, and reduce battery draw during long sessions.

📺 Native Android TV Support

Includes full controller and directional navigation support, letting you experience your favorite voxel worlds directly on the big screen.

ELI 5 Client Advanced is an open-source project based on Luanti / Minetest.
